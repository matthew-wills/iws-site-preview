Changelog

What changed, release by release

What an investigator notices in each release: the features added and the bugs fixed. Housekeeping and internal work is left out. The next release, 0.12.0, is planned and not yet released; it is described on the download page.

0.12.0Planned, not yet released
Added
  • Dashboard: completeness tiles above the checklist and the to-do list, each with a jump to the work.
  • Jump to the map: a to-do's origin, an evidence item's use or a record check takes you to the object, highlighted.
  • Timeline: time precision and zone, source, status, links to the map, phases and your own columns.
  • Evidence: drop a PDF or any file onto an object or the Evidence tab; open it in your own reader; newest first; text extracts read as extracts; your own categories; an optional attachments folder beside the investigation.
  • Figures: tick an image to carry it into the report with a caption and cross-reference links.
  • Evidence and Argument Tables document (then called the E/I/I tables): a contents page, the map, one table per page, and the evidence under each table as an option.
  • Report styles: each described with its length and an example; the map, timeline and Evidence and Argument Tables as optional appendices.
  • Probability wording: the likelihood bands and their accepted phrases adjustable in Settings; the object form shows what a slider position reads as.
  • A Findings list says when a finding stated without a likelihood term was established as fact.
  • Record before Draft report in the tab order; the Draft report page in three bands.
  • The User Guide illustrated throughout, with every control explained.
  • The maker named: Investigation Workflow Suite is made by Synoptic Safety Investigation Software.
Fixed
  • Record checks read only the sentence that restates a factor's own claim, leave excluded hypotheses and other risk factors unconnected by design, and read a numbered Findings list item by item.
  • The command-line export carries figures like the app's own export, and a figure's caption reads as its own sentence.
  • User Guide passages that named old tabs or described behaviour the app no longer has are corrected.
0.11.030 September 2026
Added
  • Six report styles: standard, three-page preliminary update, executive brief, and three final-report styles modelled on published investigation reports.
  • Repair against the audit: flagged sections sent back with the record excerpts, re-audited, and landed one at a time.
  • Probability wording per band and finding terms, adjustable in Settings; the confidence slider offers 5 and 95.
  • Evidence items as read-only cards edited in a full form, with Finalise and Reopen.
  • Interview note windows and a Finalise that renders the record as one document; Teams transcripts import from docx or vtt.
  • The investigation log records the app's own actions with a timestamp.
  • Reports carry a contents page, section page breaks and an activity appendix.
  • Signed licences with a 30-day trial; an unlicensed copy runs as the then-Standard tier with a watermark.
Fixed
  • The record check reads findings through their subheadings and compares like with like.
  • Notations from the scaffold no longer leak into report prose.
  • Word's leftover instances after a PDF run are closed by the publishing script.
0.10.029 September 2026
Added
  • Two standard builds, generic and training, each as an installer and a portable exe.
  • Interviews are a record: import a Teams transcript (docx or vtt) or type notes, keep the investigator's notes beside it, name people by role, and mark it final. Drafting uses the finalised record as the interview's evidence.
  • The checklist and interview templates are written for any investigator and edited in Settings.
  • The standard of proof presets are named by what they mean, from the balance of probabilities at 50 per cent to very likely at 90.
Fixed
  • Object forms open at about 80 per cent of the window, so a long argument is readable without resizing.
  • The Checklist tab's to-do column is wide enough to read a to-do with its source line.
0.9.127 September 2026
Added
  • Shared folders: a save that would overwrite another investigator's changes is held, with reload, copy or overwrite as the choices, and a lock file beside the open investigation tells the other person it is in use.
  • Causal map: Shift+click adds to the selection, Shift+drag draws a selection box, and the selection drags as one.
  • Object forms carry a to-do list, and every to-do appears on the Checklist tab with a link back to its object.
Fixed
  • Settings panes scroll on their own, so a long pane no longer scrolls the dialog behind it.
0.9.025 September 2026
Added
  • Editable abbreviations lists in Settings, with import, merge, export and a reset to the shipped list.
  • Glossary sets chosen per interview, so only the terms that appear in the record are sent to a model.
0.8.xSeptember 2026
Added
  • Whole-report drafting in one run, landed as one document and as sections.
  • A full-size viewer for captured evidence, and a link to the original on each safety report.
Fixed
  • Captured evidence can be deleted again.
